# 05 — Kit Library (Authoritative)

## Executive Summary
Catalog of every oral kit prescribed by HairOS, with clinical purpose, mechanism, drivers addressed, ideal profile, trigger signals (code-anchored), exclusions, outcomes, recovery timeline, narrative pointers, and product mapping. 29 active kits + Early Greying family (32 SKUs counting VEG variants); ~14 topical SKUs in a separate catalog (`src/packages/ai-engine/clinical-engine/kits/products.json`). Each kit's clinical narrative (Diagnosis Insight, Treatment Objective, Therapeutic Strategy, Formulation Rationale, Expected Clinical Response, Clinical Note) lives in `all-kits-info.txt`. Kit master register, gender / VEG gating, and condition→kit triggers are canonical in `CONDITION_KIT_MAPPING_REFERENCE.md` §1–§5. Per-kit triggers are enforced by the 16 rule files in `src/packages/ai-engine/kit-scorer/rules/`. Video blocks are dynamically generated by `src/packages/ai-engine/narrative-engine/build3DAvatarScript.ts` — no static per-kit clips exist [MISSING].

## HAIR FACT TE GOLD — Telogen Effluvium Gold Kit
**Clinical Purpose:** Stabilise active hair fall by addressing the internal triggers that disrupt the hair-growth cycle.
**Therapeutic Focus:** Five-pathway intervention — inflammation control, nutritional repletion, metabolic optimisation, hormonal modulation, stress regulation.
**Mechanism:** Bioavailable micronutrients (D, B-complex, iron support) reactivate follicular activity; amino-acid complex (Leucine/Isoleucine/Lysine) supplies keratin substrate; antioxidants (Curcumin, Moringa, Green Tea) reduce micro-inflammation; Kelp/Inositol/Fenugreek/Gymnema correct insulin/metabolism; Melatonin + Ashwagandha lower cortisol; Probiotics + Bioperine + Colostrum optimise gut absorption; Lactoferrin restores ferritin.
**Primary Drivers Addressed:** TE_STRESS, TE_NUTRITION, TE_ILLNESS, TE_POSTPREG, TE_DELIVERY.
**Secondary Drivers Addressed:** Mild AGA score contribution at chronic durations.
**Ideal Patient Profile:** Active shedding 1–3 / 3–6 months, white-bulb shedding, recent stress / nutrition / illness trigger.
**Trigger Signals:** ACTIVE_SHEDDING_TE, TE_STRESS_SCORE +55, TE_NUTRITION_SCORE +58, TE_ILLNESS_SCORE +72.
**Exclusions:** Q2 "thinning/no visible fall" (TE_GOLD_SUPPRESSION_V39 permanently suppresses); regrow-only goal; peri-menopause supersedes; GI GOLD supersedes; Pregnancy.
**Expected Outcomes:** W2–4 noticeable reduction in shedding; W6–8 improved strength + reduced variability; W10–12 visible early regrowth and cycle normalisation.
**Recovery Timeline:** 12 weeks foundational phase; reassess Phase transition at M3.
**Associated Narrative Blocks:** `all-kits-info.txt` lines 2–89 (TE GOLD block); `report-engine/v3/ingredientMechanisms.ts` (ingredient → mechanism mapping).
**Associated Video Blocks:** [MISSING] — dynamic via `build3DAvatarScript.ts`.
**Product Mapping:** TE GOLD (non-veg) / TE GOLD VEG (HR-1 swap).
**Topical Mapping:** Often paired with Minoxidil 5% (if pattern signals also present) — `products.json`.
**Source:** `kit-scorer/rules/teGoldGatingRule.ts`, `activeSheddingRule.ts`; CKM §1 Q1/Q2/Q4.
